Privacy Policy for Pocket Bhajan Diary (BDM)

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Developer / contact: Ashok Kumar Sharma & Ramesh Kumar Sharma (Sharma Brothers), bdmpocketbhajan@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Pocket Bhajan Diary (BDM) is a devotional Android app for reading the lyrics of Bhajans, Aartis, Chalisas, Stotras, the Geeta and other sacred texts. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app handles and what it does not.

2. Information we collect

The app has no accounts, registration or login. We collect personal information only if you choose to fill in the optional “Request a Bhajan” form inside the app. That form sends:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • The name of the bhajan you want
  • Lyrics and a YouTube link, only if you choose to add them

Nothing is collected automatically while you read or browse the app.

3. How we use it

We use the details from the form only to review your request and, if needed, to contact you on WhatsApp about the bhajan you asked for. We do not sell or rent this information, and we do not use it for marketing.

4. Information stored on your device

To make reading smooth, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • A saved copy of the downloaded devotional content, so it can be read later without internet
  • Your favourites list
  • Your reading theme (Morning Darshan or Evening Aarti)
  • Your reading text size

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it. It is removed if you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

5. Content download and third-party services

The app connects to these services to work:

  • Google Apps Script and Google Sheets: our online content source. The app downloads the devotional library from it.
  • Google Forms: receives the optional “Request a Bhajan” form.
  • YouTube: some items show an embedded video or a link to YouTube. YouTube and Google handle your data when you play a video.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its text fonts from Google Fonts.

When the app connects to these services, they may see your IP address, as with any internet connection. Please read Google’s privacy policy here: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Payments

The Mandir Seva page shows temple support details, a QR code, a UPI ID you can copy, and a link that opens your own UPI app. Any payment is made inside that app and with your bank. The app does not receive, process or store your payment or bank details.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking cookies or user profiling.
  • We do not use the microphone or record any audio.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs, photos or files.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or password.

8. Children

The app is a general devotional reading app suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep Request a Bhajan submissions only as long as needed to handle your request. To ask us to delete your name and WhatsApp number, email bdmpocketbhajan@gmail.com with the name and number you used, and we will delete your entry.

10. Security

The app downloads content and sends the request form over encrypted HTTPS connections. We take reasonable care of the information you send us, but no online service can promise complete security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its date at the top.
